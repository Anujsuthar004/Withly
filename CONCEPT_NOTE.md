# Withly — Focus & Coffee

## Product

Help adults find one coworking companion, study partner, or coffee buddy for a low-stakes session in a public space. The launch audience is students, remote workers, and people building side projects in one pilot area.

## Core journey

1. Confirm an email and create a profile.
2. Post a focus session or coffee walk, using a preset if helpful.
3. Browse requests and send an introduction.
4. Accept one companion, coordinate privately, and check in.
5. Submit session feedback; report or block whenever necessary.

The stored lane names `social` and `errand` remain for compatibility with existing data. The interface calls them Coworking & Focus and Coffee Walk. Discovery uses text areas and tags; it does not guarantee distance-based matching, venue verification, or a productivity improvement.

## Safety boundaries

- Adults 18+; email confirmation is not identity verification or a background check.
- Recommend public cafes, libraries, and coworking spaces. Users choose and assess their meeting place.
- A block ends active sessions together and prevents further messaging, check-ins, SOS contact, and new matches between those accounts.
- SOS is an in-app alert to the matched companion, not emergency dispatch or external-contact notification.
- Moderators need a monitored support mailbox and an operational report-review process.
- Account deletion removes avatar files and hard-deletes the account; retained messages authored by the deleted member are redacted. Limited security/moderation records and backups follow the published retention policy.

## Initial scope

One-to-one sessions, introductions, private chat, feedback, reporting/blocking, availability, and optional browser push. Group sessions, identity verification, automated emergency response, paid plans, and guaranteed geographic matching are not launch features.

## Rollout and measures

Start with a small, moderated pilot after the production release gates pass. Measure successful matches, completed sessions, repeat use, reports, and time to moderation response before expanding. See `docs/LAUNCH_HARDENING_STATUS.md` for implementation and deployment status.
